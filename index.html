CVRL, the Computer Vision Research Lab at The University of Melbourne, conducts research in computer vision, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Led by Dr Naveed Akhtar, an ARC DECRA Fellow, the lab develops interpretable, robust and responsible AI systems. We investigate adversarial machine learning, explainable AI, responsible generative AI, multi-modal and foundation models, and 3D point cloud analysis.
