CVRL, the Computer Vision Research Lab at The University of Melbourne, conducts research in computer vision, machine learning and artificial intelligence.

At the School of Information and Computing Systems at the University of Melbourne, CVRL explores how machines can see and understand the world. Computers now interpret images, video and 3D scenes in self-driving cars, medical imaging, environmental monitoring and robotics, and we want those systems to be reliable and trustworthy as well as accurate. Our research covers several connected questions, e.g., how AI systems can explain what they see, how they can withstand attempts to deceive them, and how they can reason about 3D spaces and answer questions about them in everyday language. We also study AI systems that can predict how a scene will change and plan their actions, so machines can act safely in real environments. A growing part of our work asks how advanced visual AI can be built to behave ethically and responsibly. Led by Dr Naveed Akhtar, the lab works with researchers, industry and government partners, and our work appears in leading international scientific journals and conferences.
